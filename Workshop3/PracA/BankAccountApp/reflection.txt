1. How does this reduce duplicated code?
- Inheritance reduces duplicated code because common properties and methods, such as Owner, Balance, Deposit() and Withdraw(), are defined once in the BankAccount base class and reused by the subclasses.

2. What new responsibilities does each subclass have?
- SavingsAccount.cs adds the responsibility of applying interest, while CheckingAccount.cs adds the responsibility of charging a transaction fee on withdrawals.
